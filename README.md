# James — Personal Portfolio

A personal portfolio website that presents my experience in digital advertising operations, tracking implementation and validation, creative technology, internal team tools, and AI quality evaluation.

## Overview

This portfolio is designed to show the kind of work I do: helping teams deliver reliable advertising experiences, validate tracking and conversion signals, investigate technical issues, and build lightweight tools around real internal workflow needs.

It also documents my ongoing journey in frontend and full-stack web development.

## What I showcase

### Internal team tools

I created lightweight Google Apps Script tools for internal team needs, including:

- PKT quiz app
- Team-tracking tool
- VL scheduler

These tools were built around specific workflow requirements to make coordination and information management easier. They are separate from advertising and campaign tracking work.

### Digital advertising operations

I have experience supporting advertising operations and measurement activities, including:

- Campaign tracking setup at platform, ad, and video levels
- Campaign and tracking troubleshooting
- Tracking validation and discrepancy checks
- Signal-integrity review
- Conversion API validation

### Pixel implementation

I support Pixel implementation and onboarding through:

- Manual pixel integration
- Google Tag Manager
- Shopify
- WooCommerce

My focus includes event setup, implementation checks, conversion-signal validation, and helping ensure tracking behavior matches expectations.

### Creative technology

I have built digital ad creative experiences such as:

- Static ad banners
- Animated HTML ad banners
- Video ad overlays

My core web-development skills for this work include HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

### Publisher website testing

I have contributed to publisher certification work by functionally testing websites to assess whether they are capable of running ads, checking expected tracking behavior, and reviewing discrepancies when implementation results do not match expectations.

### AI quality evaluation

I evaluate AI chatbot conversations by comparing responses with available resources and real-life human context. This work focuses on factuality, relevance, and whether the response is useful and appropriate for the conversation.
